# Hybrid Work & Workplace Conduct Handbook

## 1. Core Working Hours
- Standard company operating hours are **Sunday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM** (local office time).
- All employees must be reachable on Slack/Microsoft Teams during core collaboration hours (**10:00 AM to 3:00 PM**).

## 2. Hybrid Work Policy
- Employees may work remotely up to **2 days per week** with prior team calendar visibility.
- Mandatory in-office days apply for quarterly planning sessions, client workshops, and onboarding weeks.

## 3. Meeting & Communication Etiquette
- External emails must include the standard corporate signature block.
- Internal meetings exceeding 30 minutes require a written agenda distributed at least **4 hours** in advance.
