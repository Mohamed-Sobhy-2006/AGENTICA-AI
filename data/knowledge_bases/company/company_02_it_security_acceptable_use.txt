# Information Security & Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Access & Authentication
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory across all corporate email, VPN, GitHub, and cloud storage accounts.
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and never shared via chat or email.

## 2. Phishing & Suspicious Email Reporting
- Employees must never download unexpected attachments or click unverified links from external domains.
- Suspected phishing or BEC spoofing emails must be forwarded immediately to the IT Security & Support queue (`security@company-internal.com`).

## 3. Data Classification Handling
- **Level 1 (Public/Internal General):** Shareable internally across all staff.
- **Level 2 (Departmental Operational):** Restricted to relevant department personnel.
- **Level 3 (Confidential):** Encrypted storage required; no external forwarding without manager sign-off.
- **Level 4 (Restricted Executive):** Strictly limited to authorized leadership roles.
