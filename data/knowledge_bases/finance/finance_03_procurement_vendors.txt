# Vendor Onboarding & Procurement Controls

## 1. Vendor Verification Standards
Before issuing any Purchase Order or initiating wire transfers, new suppliers must submit:
- Valid Business Registration Certificate
- Tax Identification / W-9 or W-8BEN-E Form
- Bank Account Verification Letter on official bank letterhead

## 2. Competitive Bidding Thresholds
- **Purchases under $5,000:** Single vendor quote is acceptable.
- **Purchases $5,000 – $25,000:** Minimum of **three (3) written quotes** required.
- **Purchases above $25,000:** Formal Request for Proposal (RFP) reviewed jointly by Procurement, Legal, and Finance.

## 3. Anti-Fraud Wire Change Protocol
Any vendor request to modify bank routing or IBAN details via email must be verified via a secondary phone call using pre-registered contact numbers. Email-only bank changes are prohibited to prevent Business Email Compromise (BEC) fraud.
