# Corporate Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Scope
Applies to all full-time employees and contractors incurring business-related travel, lodging, or client meal expenses.

## 2. Per Diem & Lodging Caps
- **Domestic Meals:** Up to **$65.00** per day with itemized receipts.
- **International Meals:** Up to **$95.00** per day with itemized receipts.
- **Hotel Lodging:** Standard business room rate up to **$220.00** per night (excluding taxes).
- **Airfare:** Economy class is mandatory for flights under 6 hours; Premium Economy requires VP approval.

## 3. Submission Deadlines
- Expense claims must be submitted within **15 calendar days** of trip completion via the Finance Portal.
- Claims submitted after **30 days** require written justification from the Department Head.
- Alcohol, personal entertainment, and traffic fines are strictly non-reimbursable.
