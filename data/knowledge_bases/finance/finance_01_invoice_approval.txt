# Invoice Approval & Accounts Payable Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes standard verification and authorization workflows for incoming supplier invoices processed by the Finance Department and automated Document AI pipelines.

## 2. Three-Way Matching Requirement
All supplier invoices exceeding **$500.00** must undergo three-way matching prior to disbursement:
1. Approved Purchase Order (PO)
2. Goods Receipt Note (GRN) or Service Completion Sign-off
3. Vendor Tax Invoice showing valid tax registration and itemized line totals

## 3. Approval Authority Matrix
| Invoice Amount (USD) | Required Approver | SLA Turnaround |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to $1,000.00 | Automated Finance Agent / Junior Accountant | 1 Business Day |
| $1,000.01 – $10,000.00 | Finance Manager | 2 Business Days |
| $10,000.01 – $50,000.00 | Director of Finance | 3 Business Days |
| Above $50,000.00 | Chief Financial Officer (CFO) | 5 Business Days |

## 4. Escalation & Exception Handling
- Invoices with missing PO numbers or tax discrepancies greater than **$5.00** must be flagged as `high` priority and routed to the Accounts Payable Specialist queue.
- Duplicate invoice numbers detected against the ERP ledger are automatically rejected.
