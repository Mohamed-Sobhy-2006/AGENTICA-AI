# Recruitment, CV Screening & Onboarding Standard Operating Procedure

## 1. Candidate Evaluation Criteria
All incoming CVs processed through the HR Agent must be evaluated against objective role rubrics:
- Core technical and domain skills match (40%)
- Relevant years of hands-on experience (30%)
- Project impact and problem-solving evidence (20%)
- Education and relevant certifications (10%)

## 2. Interview Stages & SLA
1. **HR Screening:** Within 3 business days of application.
2. **Technical Assessment / Case Study:** Evaluated by hiring team within 4 business days.
3. **Final Panel Interview:** Completed within 10 business days of initial screening.

## 3. Pre-Onboarding Checklist
Once an offer letter is signed, HR initiates IT provisioning, background verification, and NDA execution at least **5 business days** prior to the candidate's start date.
