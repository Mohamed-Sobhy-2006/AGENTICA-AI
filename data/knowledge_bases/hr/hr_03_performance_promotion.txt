# Performance Appraisal & Promotion Guidelines

## 1. Review Cycles
- **Mid-Year Check-in (June):** Developmental feedback and KPI calibration.
- **Annual Performance Appraisal (December):** Formal rating on a 5-point scale (1 = Unsatisfactory to 5 = Exceptional).

## 2. Promotion Eligibility
To be nominated for promotion, an employee must:
- Hold their current role for a minimum of **12 months**.
- Achieve an annual performance rating of **4.0 or higher**.
- Have zero active disciplinary warnings during the preceding 12-month period.

## 3. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
Employees rated below **2.5** are placed on a structured **60-day Performance Improvement Plan** co-managed by their Line Manager and an HR Business Partner.
