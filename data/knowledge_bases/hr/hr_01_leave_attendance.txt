# Employee Leave & Attendance Policy

## 1. Annual Paid Leave
- Full-time employees accrue **21 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year (1.75 days per month).
- Up to **5 unused leave days** may be carried over into Q1 of the following year; excess unused days expire on March 31.
- Leave requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least **2 weeks in advance**.

## 2. Sick Leave
- Employees are entitled to up to **10 paid sick days** per year.
- Sick absences exceeding **2 consecutive working days** require a licensed physician's medical certificate attached to the HR portal request.

## 3. Parental & Bereavement Leave
- **Maternity Leave:** 14 weeks fully paid.
- **Paternity Leave:** 10 working days fully paid within 6 months of birth.
- **Bereavement Leave:** Up to 5 working days for immediate family members.
